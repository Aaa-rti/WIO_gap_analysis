Panel A shows OECM protected connectivity nodes in orange. Panel B shows MPA protected connectivity nodes in blue. Grey points represent all other WIO connectivity nodes in each panel. 

The dashed horizontal lines mark the 25th and 75th Netflow percentiles. The dashed vertical line marks the 75th percentile of indegree and identifies the most highly connected corridors.

Together, the two panels show where important larval sources, sinks, and connectivity corridors coincide with existing MPAs and OECMs across the WIO.
