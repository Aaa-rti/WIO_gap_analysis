Element 3 — areas of biodiversity and ecosystem importance.

element3_final.png

This figure summarises the representation of areas of particular importance for biodiversity and ecosystem functions within the Western Indian Ocean protected areas (MPA + OECM).

The indicators are Key Biodiversity Areas (KBAs), Important Bird Areas (IBAs), turtle nesting sites, Ecologically or Biologically Significant Marine Areas (EBSAs), and important fish larval dispersal corridors. The horizontal WIO summary bar plot shows the percentage of each indicator protected within the WIO region. The circular plot shows the corresponding percentages for individual sovereign states, with colours distinguishing the five indicators.
