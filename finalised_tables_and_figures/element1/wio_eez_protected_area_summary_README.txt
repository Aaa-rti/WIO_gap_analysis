
Element 1 — overall marine protection

wio_eez_protected_area_summary.csv


This table summarises the area of marine protection within sovereign Exclusive Economic Zones (EEZs) across the Western Indian Ocean (WIO).

For each sovereign state, the table reports total EEZ area (km²), the total protected area covered by Marine Protected Area (MPA) or Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measure (OECM) and, the separate MPA and OECM areas. Each of these areas are also expressed as a percentage of the sovereign state's EEZ. 

The total protected area value represents the spatial union of MPAs and OECMs, so locations where an MPA and OECM overlap are counted only once. Separate MPA and OECM columns are not mutually exclusive and therefore should not be added together to obtain the combined total.

The final "WIO" row provides the corresponding regional totals across the EEZs included in the analysis. The "Disputed" row represents EEZ space classified as disputed in the source EEZ dataset and was not included in further analysis.
