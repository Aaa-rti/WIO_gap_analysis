Element 4 — connectivity; Element 5 — important larval sources and sinks.

Element4and5.png

This figure combines the Western Indian Ocean connectivity and conservation sustainability assessments from Elements 4 and 5 and shows their protection proportions.

The figure summarises two connectivity indicators and two sustainability indicators: migratory connectivity derived from MiCO marine-migration connections and fish larval connectivity derived from the connectance matrix and, fish larval source areas, and fish larval sink areas. The horizontal summary plot reports the WIO-wide percentage represented within protected areas for each indicator. The circular plot presents the corresponding percentages for individual sovereign states, with separate coloured bars for each connectivity and sustainability indicator with radial values expressed from 0% to 100%.

Together, the panels show both the regional and country level protection for connectivity and sustainability proxies/indicators.
