Element 2 — ecological representativeness of habitats.

Element2_habitat_protection.jpeg

This figure summarises the ecological representativeness of four marine habitat types across Western Indian Ocean sovereign states: mangroves, coral reefs, seagrass and seamounts.

The plot on the left shows how each habitat is distributed among sovereign states within the WIO. Each habitat bar sums to 100%, and the coloured sections show the proportion of the regional habitat extent occurring within each country's EEZ. (i.e. xx% of Mangrove in the WIO is found in Kenya.)

The plot on the right shows how much of the total WIO extent of each habitat is protected (MPA + OECM), partitioned by sovereign state. Bar heights therefore represent the percentage of the entire WIO habitat extent that is protected, and the country level contributions. 

Together, the two plots show how each habitat is distributed across the WIO and which countries contribute to its current protected area representation.
